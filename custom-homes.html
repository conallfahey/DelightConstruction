LOCAL EXPERTISE

The Missoula Neighborhood Builder’s Guide

Every Missoula neighborhood builds differently. We tailor foundations, framing, and finishes to the terrain, soils, and code requirements of your lot — so your home performs for decades.

Rattlesnake & Grant Creek

Steep slopes and the Wildland‑Urban Interface (WUI) drive engineering and materials. We plan hillside foundations, slope drainage, and fire‑smart details while framing views that take in the Rattlesnake Corridor and Grant Creek hillsides.

South Hills & Miller Creek

“Missoula Blue Clay” is notorious for movement. Our foundation process accounts for expansive soils, moisture management, and control joints to prevent future cracking and heaving — a major pain point for South Hills homeowners.

Target Range & Orchard Homes

Larger lots and a semi‑rural feel call for thoughtful septic planning and site orientation. We blend new construction with the area’s agricultural history, balancing privacy, sun, and outbuilding access.

Mullan BUILD Area

We understand the city’s master plan for this 2,000‑acre expansion and build with future‑ready infrastructure in mind — siting, utilities, and access that keep pace with Missoula’s growth on the west side.

HARDENED HOMES

FireWise Construction & Wildfire Mitigation

Western Montana homes need wildfire‑smart design. We build to meet 2026 safety standards with hardened assemblies and defensible site layouts.

  • Ember‑Resistant Venting that stops attic ignition from wind‑driven embers.
  • Defensible Space “Zone 0–3” planning to reduce fuel while preserving privacy.
  • Class A Roofing and non‑combustible siding such as fiber‑cement or metal.

2026 TRENDS

Montana Modern Traditional

Design is shifting away from stark minimalism toward Warm Organic Modern — a blend of clean lines, tactile materials, and landscape‑inspired color.

  • The Palette: sage greens, muted ocher, and terra cotta that echo the Bitterroot and Sapphire ranges.
  • Curved Architecture: arched doorways and curved islands to soften circulation.
  • Biophilic Design: framing the view with glass selected for the right U‑Factor so you get the panorama without winter drafts.

ACCESSORY DWELLINGS

Maximizing Your Lot: Building ADUs in Missoula

Montana’s 2024–2026 legislation made ADUs far easier to build. Most lots now allow for an ADU up to 1,000 sq ft or up to 75% of the primary home’s size. We design ADUs for multi‑generational living or rental income — with utility planning that keeps the project smooth.

  • The Law: guided permitting, setbacks, and size compliance for Missoula lots.
  • The Use Case: flexible layouts for family, guests, or passive income.
  • The Technicals: when we can tap existing city sewer/water vs. when a new tap is required.
