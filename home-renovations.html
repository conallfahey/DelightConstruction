RENOVATIONS

Renovation Planning that Holds Together

Renovation work gets complicated when layout changes, structural updates, finish selections, and live-in logistics all overlap. We keep the scope organized from the start so decisions are made in the right order.

That may mean opening up a floor plan, updating multiple rooms, adding square footage, or tying together interior and exterior improvements under one coordinated plan.

  • Whole-home and multi-room renovations
  • Additions and expanded living space
  • Interior layout reconfiguration
  • Kitchen and bathroom coordination
  • Finish carpentry, flooring, drywall, and trim
  • Phased project planning for lived-in homes
LOCAL

Missoula Renovations with Local Context

Older homes in the Missoula area often come with layout bottlenecks, uneven surfaces, dated systems, and additions that were never fully integrated. A successful renovation has to solve those issues, not just cover them up.

We help homeowners define the right scope so the finished work feels cohesive instead of pieced together.

OUR PROCESS

A Renovation Plan Built Around Scope Control

1

Walk-Through and Goals

We review how the home works today, what needs to change, and where scope should stay focused.

2

Planning and Selections

Layout choices, finish direction, and sequence planning happen early so the renovation does not lose momentum later.

3

Demo and Rough Work

We open what needs to be opened, protect the rest of the home, and coordinate the rough work that supports the new plan.

4

Build and Finish

Framing adjustments, drywall, flooring, trim, cabinetry, tile, and paint come together under one managed timeline.

5

Final Walk-Through

We close with a punch list review, a clean handoff, and a finished space that feels intentional from room to room.
