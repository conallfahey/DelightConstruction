OUR PROCESS

A Remodel Plan That Stays on Track

1

Walk‑Through & Scope

We listen first, measure the space, and clarify what matters most: layout, storage, cooking flow, finishes, and budget priorities.

2

Selections & Pre‑Construction

Cabinets, counters, lighting, and plumbing fixtures get selected early so lead times don’t derail the schedule.

3

Demo & Prep

Dust control, protection for the rest of the home, and a safe, tidy demo. We prep framing, electrical, and plumbing the right way.

4

Build & Detail

Cabinet installs, trim, tile, and finishes handled with disciplined quality control so everything lines up clean and feels solid.

5

Final Walk‑Through

Punch list, clean handoff, and a kitchen you can use immediately — with confidence in what’s behind the walls.

FAQ

Kitchen Remodel Questions

Can we live in the home during the remodel?

Do you help with layout and design decisions?
Yes. We’ll guide layout improvements and coordinate selections so the build goes smoothly.

How do changes work once the project starts?
We document scope changes clearly so you always know what’s changing and why.
