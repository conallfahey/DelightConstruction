DECK BUILDING

Outdoor Spaces Built for Real Use

A good deck should make the yard easier to use, not create a maintenance headache. We focus on layout, structure, drainage, and finish choices that match how you want to spend time outside.

Whether you are building a brand-new deck or replacing worn materials and framing, we plan for long-term performance from the start.

  • New deck construction
  • Deck rebuilds and structural repairs
  • Stairs, landings, and rail systems
  • Composite and wood decking options
  • Drainage, flashing, and weather detailing
  • Outdoor living layouts tied to the home
LOCAL

Missoula Area Deck Projects

Decks in Western Montana need to account for snow load, sun exposure, spring runoff, and freeze-thaw cycles. Material choice matters, but so do the details underneath.

We help homeowners think through access, stairs, railing style, and how the deck connects to the house and yard before construction begins.

OUR PROCESS

A Cleaner Path from Layout to Final Board

1

Site Walk and Scope

We review access, grade changes, connection points, and how the finished deck should function.

2

Material and Detail Planning

We sort through decking material, railing choices, stair layout, and the structural details that affect longevity.

3

Framing and Structure

Posts, beams, framing, and connections are handled with attention to load, drainage, and clean lines.

4

Decking and Finish Work

Surface boards, rails, stairs, and trim details get installed with spacing and alignment that look intentional.

5

Final Walk-Through

We close out with cleanup, final review, and a deck that is ready to use right away.
