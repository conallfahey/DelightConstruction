BATHROOM REMODELS

Built for Moisture, Daily Use, and Clean Finishes

A bathroom has to do more than look good. It needs solid waterproofing behind the finishes, dependable ventilation, and materials that stay easy to maintain over time.

Whether you are updating a hall bath, reworking a primary suite, or replacing a worn-out shower, we focus on the details that make the room feel better and last longer.

  • Shower and tub remodels
  • Tile installation and waterproofing systems
  • Vanities, mirrors, and storage upgrades
  • Lighting, ventilation, and electrical coordination
  • Flooring, trim, and paint finishes
  • Clean demo and tidy jobsite protection

OUR PROCESS

A Bathroom Remodel with Fewer Surprises

1

Walk-Through and Scope

We review the layout, fixtures, moisture issues, and finish goals so the project starts with a realistic plan.

2

Selections and Pre-Construction

Tile, fixtures, glass, lighting, and vanity selections get organized early to keep the schedule moving.

3

Demo and Prep

We protect adjacent areas, remove failed materials cleanly, and prep framing, plumbing, and surfaces the right way.

4

Build and Finish

Waterproofing, tile, trim, and fixture installs are handled with quality control at each stage.

5

Final Walk-Through

We finish with punch list review, cleanup, and a bathroom that feels complete and ready to use.

FAQ

Bathroom Remodel Questions

Can you remodel a small bathroom?

Do you handle shower waterproofing and tile work?
Yes. We focus on the layers behind the tile as much as the finished surface.

Can this be part of a larger remodel?
Absolutely. Bathroom work often gets bundled into broader home renovation projects.
